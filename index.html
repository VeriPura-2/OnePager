The problem.

An analysis of 2025 import data from the FDA and TRACES determined that documentation errors drive a large share of port rejections.

40%of port rejections in the US were the result of documentation errors.FDA, 2025 import data
39%of port rejections in the EU were the result of documentation errors.TRACES, 2025 import data
  • Compliance lives in certificates, lab reports, invoices and packing lists, and errors sit between them. People read the pages, so issues surface at the border, and the importer carries the liability.
  • Rules depend on product, origin and destination, and they change continuously. The EU Forced Labour Regulation, for example, applies from 14 December 2027.

What we build.

CORE™The one engine we build. 70+ specialist AI agents, nine stages, three jobs.
